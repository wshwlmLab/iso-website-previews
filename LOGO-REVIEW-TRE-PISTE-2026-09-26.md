## Verified reference
https://www.ndc.co.jp/en/projects/tcs/
Nippon Design Center's official project page credits art direction to Yoshiaki Irobe and describes a visual system linking letters, year and area codes; the pixel unit is related to the T's thickness. The system operates both as a small mark and as an expanded key visual. These are reference principles, not ISO design decisions.
