# Il Suono Organizzato — Logo Research Canonical

Last updated: 2026-09-25

## Working method
- Proceed slowly and incrementally.
- References are reviewed one or two at a time, occasionally, without pressure to complete the list quickly.
- Do not turn every reaction into a new logo proposal.
- Preserve positive, negative, and mixed reactions as research data.
- Do not generate new logo concepts unless the user explicitly asks to move from research into design.

## Reference reactions

### 23 — Tokyo Creative Salon / Nippon Design Center
Status: STRONG POSITIVE

What the user likes:
- The way the name/letters are written and distributed as a visual composition.
- The idea of reducing a long name to selected characters/fragments and allowing those fragments to occupy the field.
- The pathways/trajectories that move through the composition.
- The paths' color is important because it gives life to the otherwise controlled composition.
- Typography is crucial to the success of the system.

Possible ISO hypothesis to preserve, NOT yet a design:
- Explore whether “IL SUONO ORGANIZZATO” could be reduced to selected consonants, initials, or word fragments and organized as a spatial composition.
- Explore paths/trajectories that connect or move around those fragments.
- Any future exploration must be original and not imitate Tokyo Creative Salon literally.
- Color may become an active element even though the website is currently primarily black/white.

### 22 — Studio Freight
Status: POSITIVE FOR ATTITUDE / MIXED FOR LOGO

What the user likes:
- The spirit and energy conveyed by the studio.
- The confidence and sense of stylistic freedom.
- Their ability to be bold without communicating instability.

What the user does NOT especially like:
- The specific three-dimensional horse/key rotating logo is not particularly appealing.

Implication:
- Use Studio Freight as a reference for attitude, confidence, and energy, not as a formal logo reference to copy.

### 24 — NPO / Studio Dumbar
Status: NEGATIVE / LOW INTEREST

Reaction:
- The user was not particularly impressed by this identity/logo.

Implication:
- Do not prioritize this formal direction in future ISO logo development.

## Important distinction
The current strongest live lead is NOT “copy Tokyo Creative Salon.”
It is:
- a long-name identity can become a spatial composition;
- selected letters/fragments may carry identity;
- a controlled typographic field can coexist with one lively path/gesture;
- typography and the path treatment must feel intentional, confident, and contemporary.

This remains one hypothesis among the broader research corpus, not the final direction.
